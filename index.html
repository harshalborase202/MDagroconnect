Seasonal Agronomy Guides

Expert tips from MD Agro Services to help you plan your soil fertilization and crop cycles strategically throughout the year.

Kharif Cycle (June - Oct)

Monsoon Sowing & Fungicides

High humidity causes fungal leaf spot and root rot in cotton and paddy seedlings.

  • Treat paddy seeds with systemic fungicide prior to transplanting.
  • Ensure field channels are clear to prevent water logging.
  • Apply vermicompost early to improve monsoon root aeration.
Rabi Cycle (Nov - April)

Winter Irrigation & Rust Defense

Wheat tillering requires balanced fertilizer dosing. Dry climate invites aphids.

  • Irrigate wheat crops at critical Crown Root Initiation (21 days).
  • Monitor crops for yellow rust powdery marks. Spray instantly.
  • Foliar spray liquid growth boosters at pre-flowering.
Summer Cycle (May - June)

Soil Preparation & Sun Care

Deep summer plowing exposes pests and pathogens to soil sterilization by solar heat.

  • Incorporate heavy vermicompost to increase soil water retention.
  • Analyze soil pH with digital meters before ordering fertilizers.
  • Keep sprinkler nozzle lines clear for hot-dry afternoons.
